Historical Chronicle

The Journey of a Visionary

01

Humble Beginnings in Rameswaram

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 into a Tamil Muslim family in the pilgrimage town of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. His father was a boat owner and imam, and his mother a homemaker. Growing up under modest financial circumstances, young Kalam distributed newspapers at dawn to support his family's household and finance his early schooling.

Despite economic hurdles, Kalam’s insatiable curiosity for flight, physics, and mathematics propelled him forward. He graduated in physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, before pursuing aerospace engineering at the prestigious Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).

02

Pioneering India's Space & Missile Sovereignty

In 1969, Kalam joined the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), serving as the Project Director of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), which successfully deployed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit in July 1980. His leadership laid the bedrock for India’s space exploration capabilities.

Transitioning to the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), he spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), overseeing the development of strategic missiles including Agni, Prithvi, Akash, and Trishul, earning him the revered moniker "Missile Man of India."

03

The 11th President of India (2002–2007)

Elected as the 11th President of India with sweeping bipartisan support in 2002, Dr. Kalam opened the doors of Rashtrapati Bhavan to common citizens, school students, and scientists. He refused ostentatious protocols and advocated tirelessly for rural infrastructure, digital empowerment, and scientific literacy under his landmark India Vision 2020 roadmap.

His term earned him the affectionate title of "The People’s President," remaining one of the most beloved heads of state in modern history.

04

An Eternal Educator & Lifelong Mentor

Following his presidency, Dr. Kalam returned to his true passion: teaching. He served as a visiting professor at IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Shillong, and IITs across India. He personally addressed over 18 million youths, inspiring them to cultivate original thinking and self-reliance.

On 27 July 2015, while doing what he loved most—delivering a lecture on "Creating a Livable Planet Earth" at IIM Shillong—Dr. Kalam collapsed and passed away. His legacy continues to ignite young minds across the globe.

Milestones of Excellence

Chronological Timeline of Achievements

1960

Graduation from Madras Institute of Technology

Completed specialized studies in Aeronautical Engineering and joined the Aeronautical Development Establishment (ADE) of DRDO.

1969

Joined ISRO as Project Director for SLV-III

Led India's first satellite launch vehicle initiative, culminating in the successful orbital deployment of the Rohini satellite in 1980.

1982

Directed IGMDP Missile Programme

Re-joined DRDO to guide the development of the Agni and Prithvi missile systems, securing India's strategic defense autonomy.

1997

Conferred the Bharat Ratna

Awarded India's highest civilian honor for immense contributions to scientific research and modernization of defense technologies.

1998

Pokhran-II Strategic Nuclear Tests

Served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister and orchestrated the Pokhran-II tests establishing nuclear deterrence.

2002

Inaugurated as the 11th President of India

Took oath as President, transforming the presidency into a vibrant hub for youth empowerment, national visioning, and science education.
