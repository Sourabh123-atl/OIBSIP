## 🎥 Step 1: How to Record Your 30–45 Second Video (Zero Setup)

According to the official Oasis Infobyte rules:
> *"The video must begin with a clear 2-second static title card displaying: (1) Full Name, (2) Assigned Track, and (3) Task Title. Afterwards, showcase the live application functioning end-to-end."*

### 🛠️ Super Easy Recording Workflow:
1. Open the **Live Master Hub**: [https://sourabh123-atl.github.io/OIBSIP/](https://sourabh123-atl.github.io/OIBSIP/)
2. Open Windows Screen Recorder: Press `Win + Alt + R` (or `Win + G` for Xbox Game Bar, or OBS / Loom).
3. Click the red **"🔴 Video Mode"** button on the task you want to record:
   - A high-tech **2-Second Title Card** appears with your name **SOURABH PATEL**, **Track**, and **Task Title** with an audio chime.
   - After 2 seconds, it automatically transitions straight into the live interactive app!
4. Interact with the app for 20-30 seconds:
   - Type input, click buttons, show responsiveness, and trigger the celebratory animations.
5. Stop recording (`Win + Alt + R`). Your video is ready to upload to LinkedIn!

## 📌 Step 3: How to Fill the Google Submission Form

1. Go to your posted video on LinkedIn.
2. Click the **three dots (`...`)** on the top-right of your post.
3. Click **"Copy link to post"**.
4. Paste the URL into the respective field in the Google Form:
   - `LinkedIn Profile link *` ➔ Your LinkedIn profile link (e.g., `https://www.linkedin.com/in/your-username/`)
   - `LinkedIn video explanation post link of task 1 *` ➔ Paste Post 1 link
   - `LinkedIn video explanation post link of task 2 *` ➔ Paste Post 2 link
   - `LinkedIn video explanation post link of task 3 *` ➔ Paste Post 3 link
   - `LinkedIn video explanation post link of task 4 *` ➔ Paste Post 4 link
   - `LinkedIn video explanation post link of task 5 *` ➔ Paste Post 5 link
5. Click **Submit**! You are 100% eligible for your **Internship Completion Certificate** and **Letter of Recommendation (LOR)**!
